OUR PRIMARY FOCUS / ITAM

We fix the data. Then we fix the process behind it.

A reliable asset program needs more than a tool. We work across physical assets, discovery data, purchasing, service management, endpoint platforms, and finance to establish trustworthy records and accountable workflows.

01↗

Hardware asset management

Know which devices exist, who has them, and what happens next.

Build and maintain device inventories across endpoints, printers, networking, peripherals, and shared equipment. Establish custody, location, lifecycle status, refresh planning, and traceable retirement.

Typical work
Receiving, tagging, and deployment controls
Owner, location, and custody reconciliation
Transfers, recovery, refresh, and disposition

RESULTA governed hardware asset register with repeatable lifecycle workflows.

02↗

Software asset & license management

Match what is deployed with what the business is entitled to use.

Reconcile installations, entitlements, publisher contracts, usage, and renewals. Identify unused allocations, reclaim licenses where permitted, and establish a defensible effective license position.

Typical work
Deployment-to-entitlement reconciliation
License harvesting and reallocation
Renewal planning and audit evidence

RESULTActionable license positions, documented exceptions, and controlled reclamation.

03↗

CMDB & data quality

Make the system of record something your team can rely on.

Define asset classes, field ownership, unique identifiers, authoritative sources, and reconciliation rules. Improve links between ITAM records, configuration items, discovery, endpoint tools, and service-management processes.

Typical work
Asset-to-CI modeling and taxonomy
Duplicate, stale, and orphan-record cleanup
Source integration and field-level governance

RESULTImproved asset and CI records with ownership and validation rules.

04↗

Inventory verification & reconciliation

Find the gaps between physical reality and recorded inventory.

Compare available inventory data against physical checks, procurement records, device discovery, endpoint management, and assigned ownership. Investigate exceptions and correct them in the designated system.

Typical work
Physical and system inventory validation
Missing, duplicate, and unassigned asset investigation
Updates directly in the designated platform

RESULTVerified records, resolved discrepancies, and repeatable audit controls.

05↗

ITAM process design & automation

Stop accurate data from becoming inaccurate again.

Design the operational controls around purchasing, receiving, onboarding, employee moves, offboarding, repair, reclamation, and disposal. Add appropriate integrations and automated updates at each lifecycle event.

Typical work
Procure-to-retire workflow design
ITSM, HR, endpoint, and procurement touchpoints
SOPs, roles, approval paths, and control checks

RESULTOwned and documented processes that keep records current.

06↗

ITAM assessment & program recovery

Find what's wrong, prioritize what matters, and put the fixes to work.

Review current tools, data sources, controls, reporting, and ownership. Establish baseline quality measures and a phased implementation plan. We can stay involved in remediation and handoff—not just deliver recommendations.

Typical work
Maturity and data quality assessment
Risk-based remediation backlog
Implementation and operational handoff

RESULTAn executable plan with verified issues, priorities, owners, and measurements.
