# OneTime Labs — Compact Redesign

## Goal
Reduce copy density and make the service offering understandable in seconds rather than through long hero sections and card-heavy pages.

## Changed
- Rebuilt the homepage as a two-choice Small Business / Enterprise screen.
- Removed the homepage bridge/mission section and long service bullet blocks.
- Rebuilt Small Business as six compact service rows.
- Rebuilt Enterprise as five compact service rows plus a small enterprise-experience strip.
- Removed support/investor promotion from the main Enterprise sales flow.
- Replaced the two-tier header and dropdown-heavy navigation with a single compact navigation bar.
- Reduced the footer to the main site destinations.
- Rebuilt Contact with a short heading and direct contact/form layout.
- Added an explicit Enterprise Consultation option to the inquiry form.
- Globally reduced hero height and headline size on existing detailed service pages.
- Preserved the existing OneTime Labs logo asset without modification.

## Validation

- CSS brace balance checked successfully.
