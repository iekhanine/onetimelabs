OneTime Labs Investor / Business Plan Redesign
September 2026

New routes
- /business
  Skim-first investor overview designed for a 7-10 second first read.
- /business/plan
  Full rewritten investor-oriented business plan.
- /business/plan?export=1
  Opens the full plan and launches the browser print dialog for Save as PDF.

What changed
- Added a prominent Investors item to the Store department navigation.
- Added a dedicated investor navigation bar: Overview / Business Plan / Export PDF.
- Reorganized the original 37-section plan into 10 investor-oriented chapters.
- Preserved the original planning assumptions, pricing ranges, revenue model,
  funding target, capital allocation, risk language, and equity/valuation caveat.
- Added Store-matched investor and business-plan styling.
- Added Letter-size print CSS designed to pack content into normal document flow.
- PDF export does not require an added npm package; it uses the browser print/save
  PDF path and temporarily sets a useful PDF filename through document.title.

PDF layout validation
- Test render: 4 US Letter pages.
- No forced one-section-per-page pagination.
- Repeated card padding and type sizes are consistent.
- Tables and compact cards are kept together where practical.
