## Positioning chosen for OneTime Labs

**Primary specialty:** ITAM, specifically HAM, SAM, asset data integrity, CMDB, inventory reconciliation, operations and implementation.

**Secondary services:** General inventory for warehouse/retail, equipment/fixed-asset tracking, managed print, vendor migration, integrations, and other software tools.

**Specific advantage supported by resume:** founder has personally worked on 25K+ asset inventories, ~$250K license reclamation, ~98% accuracy and 40K+ device environments. Avoid falsely representing these as achievements *by OneTime Labs*.

**Important distinction:** A CMDB is appropriate for configuration items and their relationships; merchandise, warehouse stock and many fixed assets belong in fit-for-purpose inventory, WMS, EAM or fixed-asset systems. Marketing uses “existing platform or new system of record”, not “everything is a CMDB”.

**Tone:** No mandatory slogan, abstract empty promises, unsupported customer case studies, invented results or vendor-partner claims. Favor outcomes and actual steps.
